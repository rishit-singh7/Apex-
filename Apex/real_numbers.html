Real Numbers

What makes a number rational, irrational or prime? Explore each idea with a friendly guide, carefully worked examples and practice you can reveal when you are ready.

A quick syllabus guide: The current CBSE Class 10 core names the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic and proofs that √2, √3 and √5 are irrational. Other useful number-system ideas are clearly labelled as revision or Olympiad extension.
