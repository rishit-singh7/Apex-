CLASS 10 · SOF IMO · SECTION 2

Quadratic Equations

What do a rectangle’s area, a parabola, and two possible answers have in common? Explore quadratic equations through visual explanations, carefully checked examples, and original Olympiad challenges.

Know what is core: The 2026–27 CBSE Class X Mathematics curriculum specifies real-root solutions by factorisation and the quadratic formula, and the relationship between the discriminant and the nature of roots. The NCERT chapter also demonstrates completing the square; it is presented here as enrichment and formula derivation, not a separately listed CBSE solving method. Root–coefficient work is labelled as a Polynomials connection or Olympiad extension.
Official references checked: CBSE Class X Mathematics 2026–27 curriculum (PDF) · NCERT Class X Mathematics, Quadratic Equations chapter · SOF IMO Class 10 syllabus and exam pattern. SOF lists Quadratic Equations in Section 2; its syllabus page does not itemise individual solution methods.
